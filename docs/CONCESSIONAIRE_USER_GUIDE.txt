STA. RITA SYSTEM - CONCESSIONAIRE USER GUIDE
=============================================

Purpose
-------
Use this guide to view your water accounts, statements, bills, payments, account-link requests, and support options.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
----------------
1. Use your own email and password. Never share your login credentials.
2. Check the account number and account name before opening a statement or making a payment.
3. Keep your SOA and valid ID files ready when requesting access to another account.
4. Log out when you finish using the portal, especially on a shared device.

1. SIGN IN
----------
Steps:
1. Open the concessionaire portal and select Sign In.
2. Enter the email address and password used during registration.
3. Select Login.
4. If you cannot sign in, confirm the email, password, and portal address. Contact the district office if the account is inactive or the password needs to be reset.

2. ACCOUNT OVERVIEW
-------------------
Purpose: See your account status, available statements, notifications, and linked accounts.

Steps:
1. Open Account Overview after signing in.
2. Review the notification bell for application, account, payment, or bill updates.
3. Review the My Accounts table to see each account number, account name, address, and status.
4. Select View Bills for an account that is approved or otherwise available.
5. Pending accounts remain visible but cannot be used for bills until approval.
6. Denied accounts cannot be used for online services. Read the denial notice and contact the district office if clarification is needed.

Important:
- Each account keeps its own account number, name, address, meter, bills, and payment history.
- One login may provide access to several accounts after each account-link request is approved.

3. STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT
-----------------------
Purpose: Review the amount due for one account without confusing it with another account.

Steps:
1. In Account Overview, open the account tab for the account you want to check.
2. Confirm the account number and address shown above the statement.
3. Check the Statement of Account date and Total Amount Due.
4. Select the statement reference to open the complete SOA.
5. Review the billing period, consumption, previous balance, discounts, penalty, and amount due.

Important:
- Statement totals are separated by account.
- A successful payment may appear on the next statement. Use Payment History to confirm previous payments.

4. BILLS AND PAYMENT HISTORY
----------------------------
Purpose: Review unpaid and paid bills for a selected account.

Steps:
1. Select View Bills for the account in Account Overview.
2. Choose Unpaid or Paid.
3. Confirm the account number in the page heading.
4. Select the receipt/view action for a bill.
5. Review the bill details before continuing to payment.
6. Use the available payment option only for the correct account and statement.

Important:
- Do not pay a bill for the wrong account.
- Do not submit payment again if the bill is already marked Paid.
- Keep the payment confirmation or receipt for your records.

5. ADD OR LINK ANOTHER ACCOUNT
------------------------------
Purpose: Request access to another existing water account using the same login.

Steps:
1. Open Account Overview.
2. Select Add Account.
3. Enter the other account number.
4. Enter the account holder name exactly as it appears in the district record.
5. Upload the latest SOA and a valid ID.
6. Select Submit Account.
7. Read the Account Verification Consent popup and select the confirmation checkbox.
8. Select I Agree to submit the request.
9. Check My Accounts for the Pending Approval status.

Important:
- Adding an account does not replace or edit the original account information.
- The original account owner, account name, meter, bills, and payment history remain unchanged.
- The linked account becomes available for bills only after district staff approve the request.
- If the name does not match, check the spelling and contact the district office if the record needs correction.

6. NEW WATER SERVICE CONNECTION
------------------------------
Purpose: Submit a request for a new water service connection.

Steps:
1. Select New Water Service Connection from Account Overview.
2. Complete the application form with accurate applicant and service information.
3. Upload the required picture, cedula, proof of billing, and any authorization document when applicable.
4. Review the application, contract, and displayed fee.
5. Submit the application and wait for the approval status.
6. For a Traverse connection, provide the required Boring/Cutting Permit when requested.

Important:
- A new connection is different from linking an existing account.
- Do not create a new connection request when you only need access to an existing account.

7. PROFILE AND PASSWORD
-----------------------
Steps:
1. Open Profile from the navigation menu.
2. Review your name, contact number, and email.
3. To change your password, enter a new password and confirmation.
4. Save the change and sign in again if requested.

Important:
- Use a strong password and do not share it.
- Contact the district office if your email or personal information needs correction.

8. SUPPORT
----------
Steps:
1. Open Submit Ticket from the navigation menu.
2. Describe the account number, bill reference, or issue clearly.
3. Include relevant dates and screenshots when helpful, but do not include your password.
4. Submit the ticket and keep the ticket details for follow-up.

9. SIGN OUT
-----------
Select Logout from the navigation menu when you finish. Always sign out on shared or public devices.

COMMON PROBLEMS
---------------
Account is pending:
The account or account-link request is still waiting for district approval. Bills and online payment remain unavailable until approval.

Account is denied:
Review the displayed reason and contact the district office. Do not submit repeated requests with incorrect information.

Statement is not showing:
Confirm that you opened the correct account tab and that the account has an available meter and bill. Pending accounts do not show usable statements.

Payment amount is different:
Confirm the account number, billing period, due date, discounts, and penalties. Contact the district office before submitting payment if the details are incorrect.
