STA. RITA SYSTEM - ADMIN AND CASHIER OPERATIONS GUIDE
====================================================

Purpose
-------
Use this guide when processing day-to-day work in the Sta. Rita system.
Only use the modules permitted by your account. Buttons and menu items may
be hidden when your role does not have access.

Before you begin
----------------
1. Sign in using your assigned account. Do not share your password.
2. Confirm the correct account number, customer name, amount, and billing
   period before saving any transaction.
3. Use Search, filters, and the date/month selector before opening a record.
4. Review success or error messages after every save.
5. Do not refresh or close the page while a save, upload, or download is
   still in progress.
6. Log out when leaving the workstation.

ROLE QUICK REFERENCE
--------------------
Cashier
- Dashboard, Payments, Installment, Billing Adjustment, Penalty Exemption,
  Online Payments, Files/Reports, and Concessionaires.

Administrator
- All cashier modules plus Meter Reading, Reading Report, Change Readings,
  Offline Readings, Reading Dates, Registrants, Imports, and Settings.
- Personnel management is normally limited to Superadmin accounts.

1. DASHBOARD
------------
Purpose: Review the current system summary before starting work.

Steps:
1. Open Dashboard from the top navigation.
2. Review totals, alerts, and any cards relevant to your shift.
3. If a figure appears unexpected, open the related module and filter by
   account number, zone, or month before making changes.

2. METER READING (ADMIN)
------------------------
Purpose: Enter a current meter reading and create the related bill.

Steps:
1. Open Meter Reading > Meter Reading.
2. Search for and select the correct account/meter.
3. Verify the previous reading and the reading date.
4. Enter the present reading exactly as recorded.
5. Review the calculated consumption and all displayed details.
6. Submit the reading once.
7. Open the generated bill/invoice if you need to verify the amount or print
   the document.

Important:
- Never enter a reading for the wrong account or an earlier billing period.
- If the meter reading must be corrected after saving, use Change Readings;
  do not create a duplicate reading.

Problem checks:
- Account not found: verify the account number and concessionaire record.
- Incorrect consumption: verify previous and present readings, then use
  Change Readings if the reading was already saved.
- No bill generated: confirm the reading was submitted successfully and ask
  an administrator to review the account/rate configuration.

3. READING REPORT (ADMIN)
-------------------------
Purpose: Review readings that were entered for a selected period.

Steps:
1. Open Meter Reading > Reading Report.
2. Set the available period, zone, or search filters.
3. Review account number, reading values, consumption, and status.
4. Use the report for checking work before billing/payment processing.

4. CHANGE READINGS (ADMIN)
--------------------------
Purpose: Correct an existing reading using the controlled adjustment screen.

Steps:
1. Open Meter Reading > Change Readings.
2. Search/filter to locate the exact reading record.
3. Open Edit for that record.
4. Compare the stored values with the approved source reading.
5. Enter the corrected value and save.
6. Re-open the bill/reading report and confirm the corrected result.

Important: Changes to readings affect bills. Obtain the required approval
before making a correction and do not alter a record merely to resolve a
payment inquiry.

5. OFFLINE READINGS (ADMIN)
--------------------------
Purpose: Review readings submitted by offline technicians before they are
merged into normal readings and bills.

Steps:
1. Open Meter Reading > Offline Readings.
2. Review pending rows and their account numbers, readings, and dates.
3. Resolve account/property-type errors before merging.
4. Use the merge/sync process available to authorized administrators.
5. Confirm the resulting reading and bill in Reading Report/Payments.

Important: Merge only once. A successful merge marks the offline row as
processed. Do not repeatedly run a merge to fix an incorrect reading; use
Change Readings instead.

6. READING DATES (ADMIN)
------------------------
Purpose: Maintain allowed reading/billing date schedules.

Steps:
1. Open Meter Reading > Reading Dates.
2. Review existing schedules first to avoid duplicates.
3. Add, edit, or delete the required date entry.
4. Verify the date, zone, and period before saving.

7. PAYMENTS (ADMIN/CASHIER)
---------------------------
Purpose: Find bills, accept walk-in payment, and print an official receipt.

Steps:
1. Open Payments > Payments.
2. Use Search (account number or name), Zone, Reading Month, Payment Method,
   and Paid/Unpaid filters to find the bill.
3. Confirm the account name, billing period, reference number, total amount,
   and unpaid status.
4. For an unpaid bill, click the payment/card action.
5. Enter the payment details exactly as received and review them.
6. Submit the payment once and wait for the success message.
7. Print or open the official receipt using the receipt action after payment.

Important:
- Do not accept a second payment for a bill already marked Paid.
- Do not change payment method or amount simply to match a customer claim;
  verify the supporting document first.

Problem checks:
- Bill cannot be found: search by account number and verify the reading month.
- Amount differs from the customer copy: review the bill breakdown and due
  date; escalate if an adjustment is required.
- Payment saved but receipt is unavailable: re-open the paid bill and use the
  receipt action. Do not process the payment again.

8. APPLICATION FEES (ADMIN/CASHIER)
-----------------------------------
Purpose: Process fees for new service-connection applications.

Steps:
1. From Payments, select Application Fees.
2. Locate the correct application and confirm applicant details/status.
3. Open the payment action for that application.
4. Verify the required fee and payment details.
5. Save once and issue the available receipt/proof.
6. Confirm the application payment status changed before proceeding with
   approval-related work.

9. INSTALLMENT (ADMIN/CASHIER)
------------------------------
Purpose: Create and maintain payment schedules for an eligible bill.

Create an installment:
1. Open Payments > Installment.
2. Click Create Installment.
3. Search using the account number or customer name.
4. Select the correct bill/reference number.
5. Confirm the bill amount, number of months, monthly amount, and first due
   date.
6. Save the schedule.
7. Use View to check every generated due date and amount.

Maintain an installment:
1. Find the installment in the list.
2. Use View to inspect the schedule.
3. Use Edit only with approved changes, then re-check the schedule.
4. Use Delete only when the installment was created in error and approval was
   obtained. Deletion is not a substitute for recording payment.

10. BILLING ADJUSTMENT (ADMIN/CASHIER)
--------------------------------------
Purpose: Apply an approved correction to billing data.

Steps:
1. Open Payments > Billing Adjustment.
2. Search/filter for the correct account and billing record.
3. Review the original bill and the approved adjustment basis.
4. Enter only the authorized adjustment.
5. Save and re-check the recalculated bill.
6. Record the reason/approval outside the system according to office policy.

11. PENALTY EXEMPTION (ADMIN/CASHIER)
-------------------------------------
Purpose: Record an approved penalty exemption.

Steps:
1. Open Payments > Penalty Exemption.
2. Locate the customer/bill.
3. Verify eligibility and approval documentation.
4. Add or update the exemption details, including the valid period/reason if
   prompted.
5. Save and verify the bill now reflects the approved exemption.

12. ONLINE PAYMENTS (ADMIN/CASHIER)
-----------------------------------
Purpose: Monitor payments received through the online payment channel.

Steps:
1. Open Payments > Online Payments.
2. Filter/search for the reference number, account, or date.
3. Confirm the payment provider status and amount.
4. Use the available sync/refresh action only when authorized.
5. Verify that the related bill shows the correct paid status before advising
   the customer.

Problem checks:
- Do not manually mark a bill paid from an unverified online transaction.
- If the payment provider says paid but the bill is unpaid, capture the
  reference number and escalate for synchronization review.

13. FILES / DOWNLOAD REPORTS (ADMIN/CASHIER)
--------------------------------------------
Purpose: Generate downloadable operational reports.

Steps:
1. Open Files from the top navigation.
2. Choose the needed report type (for example, ageing, penalty, franchise
   tax, or disconnected concessionaires).
3. Set all required date/month, zone, and other filters.
4. Re-check the filters because they determine the file contents.
5. Click Generate/Download and wait for the browser download to finish.
6. Open the file and check the title, period, and row count before sending it
   to another office.

If a download is empty:
1. Check the selected date/month and filters.
2. Try a broader filter once.
3. Confirm there is data in Payments/Reading Report for that period.
4. Report the chosen filters and expected data to the administrator if the
   file remains empty.

14. CONCESSIONAIRES (ADMIN/CASHIER)
-----------------------------------
Purpose: Search and maintain concessionaire account information.

Steps:
1. Open Concessionaires.
2. Search using account number, name, zone, or the available filters.
3. Open the correct record and verify the customer identity before editing.
4. Update only authorized fields.
5. Save, then re-open the record to confirm the change.
6. Use the ledger option, where available, to review account activity rather
   than guessing a balance from a single bill.

15. REGISTRANTS AND SERVICE APPLICATIONS (ADMIN)
------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Review new registrations and connection applications.

Steps:
1. Open Users > Registrants.
2. Filter by status and registrant type (Existing Account or New Connection).
3. Verify the applicant details and documents.
4. For a new connection, open Form and Contract as needed.
5. If details are incomplete, use Complete and save the missing information.
6. Confirm application fee/payment requirements.
7. For Traverse connections, verify the Boring/Cutting Permit before approval.
8. Click Approve only after all requirements are complete; use Deny only with
   a clear reason.

16. IMPORTS (ADMIN)
-------------------
Purpose: Import approved master/billing data.

Global Information:
1. Open Users > Import > Global Informations.
2. Use the required template and check column names/order before upload.
3. Make a backup of the source file.
4. Upload the file and review validation errors before retrying.

Previous Billing:
1. Open Payments > Payments > Upload Billing, or Users > Import > Previous
   Billing.
2. Select the approved previous-billing file.
3. Confirm the target billing period and file before submitting.
4. Review import results/errors and check several imported accounts.

Never upload the same source file repeatedly without first confirming whether
the previous attempt completed.

17. SETTINGS (ADMIN)
--------------------
Purpose: Maintain configuration that affects billing.

Available settings include Property Types, Base Rate, Water Rates, and Payment
Breakdown.

Steps:
1. Open Users > Settings and select the setting.
2. Review current entries and identify the exact item to change.
3. Confirm the new value and its effective use with an authorized supervisor.
4. Save the change once.
5. Verify the saved value and test/review a non-production example where your
   process allows.

Warning: Rate and payment-breakdown changes can affect many future bills.
Never change these settings to correct one customer's bill; use Billing
Adjustment instead.

18. ROLES AND PERSONNEL (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN)
------------------------------------------
Purpose: Manage access, not customer billing.

Steps:
1. Open Users > Roles or Users > Personnels.
2. Confirm the staff member and required role before making a change.
3. Apply the least access necessary for the job.
4. Save and have the staff member sign out/in before testing access.
5. Remove access promptly when duties change.

19. SUPPORT TICKETS AND PROFILE
-------------------------------
Support Ticket:
1. Click Submit Ticket.
2. Describe the issue, account/reference number (if applicable), time, and
   steps already tried.
3. Do not include passwords or full payment-card details.
4. Submit and retain the ticket reference.

Profile:
1. Open Profile from the top navigation.
2. Update only your own authorized profile details.
3. Use a strong, unique password when changing it.

COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------
1. "No data found"
   - Clear Search, verify filters, select the correct month, and retry.

2. A table is wider than the screen
   - Scroll horizontally within the table. Use a larger screen if you need to
     review many columns at once.

3. A save fails
   - Read the validation message, correct the highlighted field, and submit
     once. Do not double-click the save button.

4. You cannot see a menu or action
   - It may be restricted by your role. Ask an administrator; do not use
     another staff member's account.

5. A bill/payment appears incorrect
   - Stop before making another transaction. Record the account number,
     reference number, displayed amount, and screenshot/error message; then
     escalate through a support ticket or supervisor.

END OF GUIDE
